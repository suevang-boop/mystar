# The 1-Minute Human Test

**Goal:** Watch one real person use your page for 60 seconds and learn what breaks.

You do NOT explain the page. Don't point at things. Don't help. Just watch.

## Before you start

- Open the live URL on your phone or laptop: https://adriancongo-neptunejames-3000.codio.io/
- Pick a peer who has NOT seen your page yet.
- Have a timer and a way to take notes ready.

## The test (60 seconds)

1. Hand over your screen and say exactly: **"Just look at this for a minute and tell me what you think it is."**
2. Start the timer. Say nothing for 60 seconds.
3. Watch their face and finger. Note where their eyes go and what they try to click.
4. When the timer ends, ask these three questions out loud:
   - **"In one sentence, what is this for?"**
   - **"Who do you think it's for?"**
   - **"What would you expect to happen if you clicked the gold button?"**

## What to write down (fill this in)

| Thing to watch | What you saw |

| Did they find the gold button without help? | |

| Any word or phrase they paused on? | |
| What they expected the gold button to do | |

## How to read the result

- **Confused "what is this?"** → your hero headline or subheadline needs to be clearer.
- **Clicked a dead link** → that link needs a real destination (see Launch Checklist).
- **Wrong user guessed** → your headline speaks to the wrong group.
- **Loved it, no confusion** → you're ready to fix the top 3 issues and ship.

## Then triage

Write down every issue, then mark the **3 most critical** ones and fix only those first.
Everything else goes on a "later" list — do not fix it all at once.
