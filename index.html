Paste a note, a scenario, or an example below and press Form STAR. The page sorts it into Situation · Task · Action · Result — each part shows what to include — then you move and edit the pieces until it reads right.

S Situation

Set the scene & context · 15–20% of your answer

  • Where were you working or studying?
  • What was the context or background of the challenge?
  • Who was affected?

T Task

Define your responsibility or challenge · 10–15% of your answer

  • What specific problem needed to be solved?
  • What was your individual goal or responsibility?
  • What were the deadlines, KPIs, or standards of success?

A Action

Detail the exact steps you took · 50–60% of your answer

  • What tools, methods, or strategies did you use?
  • How did you handle roadblocks or disagreements?
  • Why did you choose that approach over others?

R Result

Quantify the outcome & lessons learned · 15–20% of your answer

  • What was the quantifiable impact (%, $, hours)?
  • What feedback did you receive?
  • What lesson did you take away?
